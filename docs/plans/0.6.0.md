## Contracts the briefs must carry

- **The caustic** (#72): the dark full step only; one ellipse centred under the sphere, (120, 233) rx 84 ry 8.5, a
  radial gradient gold `#ffcf7a` (.9), orange `#ff9a5c` (.55), pink `#ff5fa2` (.2) to clear, blurred with a standard
  deviation of 5 through its own filter. The shared `blur4` stays for light. No dark contact shadow. Light unchanged.
- **The frame** (#72): the full step's frame grows downwards only, about 240 × 260, its final height taken from the
  rendered glow, not the 256.5 estimate; the same in both themes. `boxSize` stays 240 as the drawing and lockup
  reference; the sphere's position and size, the steps' alignment and the breakpoints don't move. The lockup's
  wordmark keeps its size, gap and vertical alignment, centred against the 240 box. Every frame that clips today (the
  emblem file, the lockup's viewport, the slot canvas, the studio's previews) must agree; anything that assumes a
  square must not shrink the bubble. Placement is pinned by the 240 box, not the taller frame: `SlotSvg` centres its
  content's box, so the Meetup photo's logo would otherwise rise about 12 px; the glow hangs below where the logo sits
  today (the lockup's `paintedBounds` already carries painted extent apart from layout). Among the assets, the two
  bubble SVGs, the two logo SVGs and the Meetup photo change; the wordmarks, avatar, Apple touch icon and favicon
  stay byte-identical.
- **Reference:** the caustic and the light shadow's newly visible tail are departures, recorded in the reference's
  README; the captures stay. The pixel test reframes the reference and allows only the recorded departures, never a
  looser tolerance.
- **Contrast** (D9, 3:1): confirmed unchanged by the existing tests (agent decides, #72).
- **Band and corners** (#66): studio options on the Meetup photo, both Off by default. The studio has no slot download
  today, so P2 adds one: SVG, and a 1200 × 675 PNG from it (what Meetup accepts), both following the toggles, from the
  same option values as the preview. Today's preview rounds the Meetup photo with a CSS mask, which would hide Corners
  Off; the preview shows the actual output unmasked, with any simulated crop kept separate. Rounded corners are
  transparent in the export. All ten combinations are checked through real downloads (size, self-contained ids), and
  the committed Meetup photo stays plain, square-cornered. The band uses `sunsetPlum` to `sunsetGold` in order; its width
  and the corner radius are the agent's, shown to the maintainer at real size before merging.
- **Slow tests** (#54): only the two tests' cost or timeout change, never what they assert or how many cases they
  sample. Record each test's duration before and after; the reported slow-machine timings are 8.1-8.8 s and 5.5 s, so

- **The quote:** the maintainer writes it; P4 places it under the title in the format of londontypescript/.github#1.
- **Verification:** the built studio in both themes; the exported SVGs as standalone `<img>` and resvg PNGs; the

## Decisions

Made with the maintainer on 31 October 2026, before this plan's pull request.

- **D1, the dark ground:** a warm caustic replaces the white smudge, centred, with no contact shadow (#72).
- **D2, the frame:** the full step's frame grows taller so the glow fades out instead of ending in a straight line,
  chosen after a clipped against roomy comparison on the Meetup photo and README header (#72). Shrinking or raising
  the bubble, a dark-only frame and visible overflow were rejected.
- **D3, the band:** a studio toggle on the Meetup photo, Off by default, with a separate rounded-corners toggle; no
  band in the committed file (#66).
- **D4, no slides** or slide templates.
- **D5, recipe names stay:** the GitHub avatar and Meetup photo are what's in use; no generic renaming.
- **D6, no brand distribution:** no hosting, tokens or brand files; files are taken from the studio by hand while the
  maintainer is LTS GitHub's only user.
- **D7, the quote:** every LTS README opens with the organiser's quote, tracked across repos by
  londontypescript/.github#1; tate-modern's is #53.
